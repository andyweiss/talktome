# Talktome Intercom support

Talktome Intercom is an iPhone and iPad client for a Talktome server operated
by you or your organisation. The app does not include a hosted intercom
service. Your server administrator provides the server address, account or
personal sign-in link and assigns your talk targets.

## Contact

Email [talktome@platonstudio.de](mailto:talktome@platonstudio.de) for app
support or privacy enquiries. Describe your device, iOS version, app version
and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords, active
sign-in links, access tokens or unredacted QR codes.

You can also [report a bug on GitHub](https://github.com/thepoison606/talktome/issues)
or join the [Talktome community on Discord](https://discord.gg/x5Q27F7HK).

## Connecting to your server

Enter the server's HTTPS address and your account details, or scan a server
or personal sign-in QR code. On a local network, allow Talktome's local
network access in iOS Settings. A camera permission is needed only to scan
QR codes. Microphone access is needed for talking.

If your server uses a self-signed certificate, compare its fingerprint with
your administrator before approving it in the app. A remote server must be
reachable from the network you are currently using.

## Audio and accessories

Choose the audio output using the speaker control in the operator screen.
Check the selected output and system volume if you cannot hear others.
Bluetooth push-to-talk accessories are optional and require a compatible
device. The Apple Watch app remotely controls the connected iPhone session;
intercom audio remains on the iPhone and its audio accessories.

## Profiles and privacy

Profiles can be removed in the app's profile list. Removing a local profile
does not delete your server account; contact your server administrator for
server-side access or deletion requests.

Read the [privacy policy](https://platonstudio.de/talktome/privacy/)
([Deutsch](https://platonstudio.de/talktome/privacy/de/)) for details about
local storage, server connections, permissions and your privacy rights.
